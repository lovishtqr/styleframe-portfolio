Good to Know

Bride questions, answered

Do you travel to the venue? +
Yes — across the Tricity, and outstation on request. Travel is charged separately outside Panchkula.
Is a trial included? +
The Signature package includes a full trial. For Essential, a trial can be added — and it's adjusted in your final payment.
HD or airbrush — which should I choose? +
At your consultation we assess your skin and the venue lighting. Oily skin + outdoor venue usually loves airbrush; dry skin often glows more in HD.
How do I lock my wedding date? +
DM us on Instagram with your date — 30% advance confirms it. We take limited bridal dates each month, so earlier is safer.
Do you do family / bridesmaid makeup? +
Yes — ₹3,500 per person for family looks, with a group discount for 4+.
